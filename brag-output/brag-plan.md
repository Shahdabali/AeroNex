# AeroNex — brag plan

**What:** Airfare intelligence for India's domestic routes — fares scraped, validated, stored with history, shown with an honest freshness label.
**For:** Anyone tracking Indian airfares (travellers, analysts, CPI work).
**Sets it apart:** "Nothing on screen is generated" — every number traces to a timestamped observation, and the app admits when data is stale.
**Funniest true claim:** an airfare app that proudly tells you when its prices are old.
**Tone:** polished / cinematic-lite. Dark navy #090A0F, cyan #00E5FF → blue #1788FF, Inter 800 uppercase.
**Caption:** Every fare has a receipt.
